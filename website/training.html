How it works

Built the way delivery teams work.

Live projects

You receive requirements and a deadline, then deliver. Your work is reviewed and sent back with comments, as it would be on a real team.

One to one sessions

Your trainer works only with you. There is no batch to keep up with, and no question is too basic to ask.

Industry habits

Version control, documentation, status reports and handover. The working habits that carry you through probation.

Trainers from the field

Each track is taught by someone who has worked in that role. Engineers teach engineering.

Honest feedback

You are told clearly where you stand and what needs work, well before an interview.

Support after training

Resume writing, interview preparation and job support continue once the sessions end.

Information technology

IT tracks.

Software and web development

Front end, back end and full stack. You build and ship a working application, handle change requests, and learn to read code you did not write.

Cyber security

Monitoring, assessment, hardening and incident handling, with the reporting discipline security roles are judged on.

Cloud and DevOps

Deployment, build pipelines, environments and monitoring. You take an application from a laptop to a running service.

Testing and quality

Manual and automation testing on live builds. Test cases that hold up and defect reports developers can act on.

Data and analytics

Questions

Before you ask.

I have no experience. Can I join?
Yes. The training is designed for people with no experience. You finish with real project work to talk about and a clear idea of how the job is done.
Is it really one to one?
Yes. Each session is one trainer and one learner, so the pace follows you.
Do you train only IT roles?
No. We also run tracks for electrical, mechanical, design and other non IT roles, taught by trainers from those fields.
What happens after training?
We help with your resume and interview preparation, and job support is available once you are placed.
How long does a track take?
It depends on your field, your starting point and the time you can give each week. We work that out with you on the first call.
